# 📱 Google Play Store Data Study

This project explores and analyzes Google Play Store data to gain insights into app categories, ratings, installs, and revenue trends.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
- **Objective:** Understand factors influencing app success on the Play Store.
- **Dataset:** Google Play Store dataset (apps, categories, ratings, installs, and pricing).
- **Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- 📈 Categories like **Games** and **Productivity** dominate in install counts.
- ⭐ **Higher ratings** correlate with better user retention.
- 💵 Free apps generate more installs, while paid apps show niche engagement.
