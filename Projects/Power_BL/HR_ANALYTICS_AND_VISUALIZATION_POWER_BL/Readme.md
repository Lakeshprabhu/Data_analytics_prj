🧭 HR Analytics Dashboard — Employee Attendance & Work Status (Power BI)
📘 Overview

This project presents an HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI, designed to monitor and analyze employee attendance, work status, and activity trends across specific time periods.
It helps HR managers and team leads visualize workforce patterns, identify absenteeism trends, and ensure optimized work distribution across departments.

🎯 Objectives

Track employee presence and absence over a given date range.

Visualize attendance trends, including late logins, remote work, and leave patterns.

Analyze work type distribution (onsite, remote, hybrid).

Provide a quick overview of department-level attendance statistics.

Identify high and low engagement periods throughout the year.
